<h1 align="left">Boardrooms App
</h1>

<h2 align="left">An iOS application that simplifies boardroom scheduling by enabling users to view room availability, book meeting rooms on specific dates, and manage their bookings efficiently.
</h2>


<h2 align="left">App features: </h2>

<ul>
 Meeting Room Availability Overview
</li>
  <li>View the full list of meeting rooms with their availability status (available / unavailable)
</li>
  <li>P Book a Meeting Room 
</li>
   <li>Book a meeting room for a specific date in a simple and intuitive way.
</li>
<li>View all scheduled and upcoming meeting room bookings in one place.
</li>
<li> My Bookings
</li>
<li> Room Details & Booking Screen
</li>
<li> My Bookings Screen
</li>
<li>Optimized for iPhone 14
</li>
</ul>
