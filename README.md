<h1> Boardrooms</h1>
<h3>
An iOS application that simplifies boardroom scheduling by enabling 
users to view room availability, book meeting rooms on specific dates, 
and manage their bookings efficiently.
</h3>
<p>
Streamlining meeting room management through a clean, 
intuitive mobile experience.
</p>
</div>

<h1>About The Project</h1>
<p>
Boardrooms is an iOS app that removes the friction from office 
room booking. Users can instantly see which rooms are available, 
book a room for a specific date, and manage all their upcoming 
reservations in one place — without needing to contact anyone 
or use a separate system.
</p>

<h1>Features</h1>
<ul>
<li>Meeting room availability overview — see all rooms and their current status</li>
<li>Book a meeting room for a specific date simply and intuitively</li>
<li>View all scheduled and upcoming bookings in one place</li>
<li>My Bookings screen for managing personal reservations</li>
<li>Room details and booking screen with full room information</li>
<li>Clean and modern iOS interface</li>
</ul>
