Boardrooms App
Overview:
Boardrooms App is an iOS application designed to simplify and organize the process of scheduling boardrooms within an organization.
The app provides a clean and user-friendly interface that allows users to easily check room availability, book meeting rooms, and manage their reservations.

App Statement
An iOS application that simplifies boardroom scheduling by enabling users to view room availability, book meeting rooms on specific dates, and manage their bookings efficiently.

Features
 Meeting Room Availability Overview
View the full list of meeting rooms with their availability status (available / unavailable).
 Book a Meeting Room
Book a meeting room for a specific date in a simple and intuitive way.
 My Bookings
View all scheduled and upcoming meeting room bookings in one place.
 App Screens

 Tech Stack
Platform: iOS
Language: Swift
Framework: SwiftUI
Architecture: MVVM
Development Tool: Xcode

 Architecture
The app follows the MVVM (Model–View–ViewModel) architecture to ensure:
Clear separation of concerns
Scalability and maintainability
Clean and testable codebase
